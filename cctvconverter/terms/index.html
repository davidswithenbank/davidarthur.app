3. Offline operation

CCTV Converter runs entirely on your computer. It has no cloud services, no accounts and no analytics. Your footage is read, converted, and written locally and never leaves your device. The only network request the App makes is an update check: automatically about once a day when it starts, and whenever you choose “Check for updates”. The Microsoft Store edition asks the Store’s own update service; the direct-download edition requests a small static version file from davidarthur.app. An update check transmits none of your settings, files or footage, and nothing is downloaded or installed without your consent. The App makes no other network requests.

4. Bundled ffmpeg

CCTV Converter relies on a bundled copy of ffmpeg for video format detection and conversion. ffmpeg is distributed under its own open-source licence (LGPL/GPL); your use of CCTV Converter is also subject to those licence terms.

5. Accuracy and limitations

CCTV Converter detects video formats from signature bytes and converts to standard MP4. It supports Xiongmai IMKH, Dahua DHAV, Hikvision, raw H.264/H.265 and common video containers including MP4/MOV, AVI, Matroska/WebM, MPEG-TS, MPEG-PS, ASF/WMV and Flash Video, covering 27 file extensions in total. Footage from some systems (for example Bosch, Axis and Milestone) is identified but needs the vendor’s own player. Vendor-encrypted formats — for example Hikvision .crypt backups — are not recognised by any third-party tool: the App does not list them, so they will not appear among your files. Files it does list but cannot decode are marked “Cannot convert”. The optional date/time overlay reads each file's modification timestamp and is provided for convenience; if accurate evidence-grade timestamping is critical, verify the source timestamps independently. Converted, edited, or trimmed output should not be relied upon as the sole forensic or legal record; where footage may be used for insurance, legal, or law-enforcement purposes, always retain and verify the original source files.
