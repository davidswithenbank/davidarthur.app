3. Offline operation

CCTV Converter runs entirely on your computer. It has no accounts. Your footage is read, converted, and written locally and never leaves your device. The App goes online to check for updates (automatically each time it starts, and whenever you choose “Check for updates”), and, for CCTV Converter Pro, to make a purchase you start and to check your Pro licence with the Microsoft Store or, for card and PayPal purchases, with our licence server; a coming update will also send crash reports and one short “app opened” note a day and ask which special offers apply, as described in our privacy policy; none of these sends anything from your footage or files. The Microsoft Store edition asks the Store’s own update service; the direct-download edition requests a small static version file from davidarthur.app. An update check transmits none of your settings, files or footage. You can skip an optional update; a required update installs by itself. Pro keeps working offline for a period after its last successful check. The App makes no other network requests.

5. Accuracy and limitations

CCTV Converter detects video formats from signature bytes and converts to standard MP4. It supports Xiongmai IMKH, Dahua DHAV, Hikvision, raw H.264/H.265 and common video containers including MP4/MOV, AVI, Matroska/WebM, MPEG-TS, MPEG-PS, ASF/WMV and Flash Video, covering 27 file extensions in total. Footage from some systems (for example Bosch, Axis and Milestone) is identified but needs the vendor’s own player. Vendor-encrypted formats — for example Hikvision .crypt backups — can’t be read by CCTV Converter: the App does not list them, so they will not appear among your files. Files it does list but cannot decode are marked “Cannot convert”. The optional date/time overlay reads each file's modification timestamp and is provided for convenience; if accurate evidence-grade timestamping is critical, verify the source timestamps independently. Converted, edited, or trimmed output should not be relied upon as the sole forensic or legal record; where footage may be used for insurance, legal, or law-enforcement purposes, always retain and verify the original source files.
