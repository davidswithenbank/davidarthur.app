Privacy Policy for CCTV Converter

Last updated: 18 November 2026

David Arthur Software ("we", "us", "our") publishes the CCTV Converter app ("the App"), a Windows utility that is free to download and use, with an optional paid upgrade, CCTV Converter Pro. This page describes how the App handles your information.

1. The short version

CCTV Converter does not collect, transmit, or share your footage or anything about what you do with it. It has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. Everything you do in the App stays on your computer. The only network activity is the update check and, for CCTV Converter Pro, the purchase and licence checks described in section 7; none of them sends anything about your footage. If you buy Pro, Microsoft or our online reseller Paddle handles the payment, as described in section 7. This includes the face-blurring tools added in version 2.1: faces are detected and hidden entirely on your device, and no facial or biometric data is ever created, stored, or sent anywhere.

2. What the App stores locally

The App writes a small configuration file at %LOCALAPPDATA%\CctvConverter\config.json on your machine. It records your settings and preferences, including:

  • The last source folder you scanned and the last output folder you chose (so the paths appear next time you open the App)
  • Up to five recent source folders and five recent output folders, for the ▼ quick-pick menus beside those fields
  • Your "Delete source files after successful conversion" checkbox state
  • Your "Add timestamp overlay to converted video" checkbox state
  • Your chosen number of parallel conversions (1 to 16)
  • Your chosen interface language (or the language auto-detected from your Windows settings on first launch)
  • A count of how many files you have successfully converted, used only to know when to offer a one-time "rate the app on the Microsoft Store" prompt, and a flag recording your reply to that prompt
  • Your other app settings, such as the encoder, file naming, watch-folder, motion-highlight, window-size and volume choices, and any frame rate you set for a raw file under "Recorded at" (stored with that file's path)
  • The time of the last update check
  • Your CCTV Converter Pro status: whether this PC has Pro (and which plan), when it was last confirmed, and, if you used the App before Pro existed, when your free month ends. It is kept in an encrypted file (pro.dat) in the same folder, protected by Windows for your user account. If you bought Pro with card or PayPal, the file also holds a random install identifier and the licence our server issued to it (see section 7).

It also caches video thumbnails at %LOCALAPPDATA%\CctvConverter\thumbs\ to make repeat browsing faster, and similar caches for the preview filmstrip, audio waveforms and motion-detection results; old cache entries are cleared out automatically. If something goes wrong, it writes an error log in the same folder. The Microsoft Store edition keeps this folder inside its own app package folder; About → Open log + cache folder opens it.

None of this data is transmitted off your device. You can delete it any time by removing the %LOCALAPPDATA%\CctvConverter\ folder.

3. Your video files

Your CCTV footage is your data. The App reads the source files you point it at, writes converted MP4s to the output folder you choose, and never sends anything anywhere. Conversions happen entirely on your computer using a bundled copy of ffmpeg.

4. Face detection and redaction

CCTV Converter can optionally detect faces in a clip so that you can blur, pixelate or black them out before you share the footage. This is a privacy feature, and like everything else in the App it works entirely on your own computer, offline.

  • Face detection runs locally, using a bundled, offline model. No image, frame, or clip is ever uploaded.
  • It is not facial recognition and does not identify anyone: the App only locates a face so you can hide it — it does not match, name, or look anyone up.
  • The App does not create, store, or transmit any facial templates, biometric data, or other information about the people in your footage.
  • You choose which detected faces to hide, and can add or remove boxes by hand. The redaction is written into the new file you export; your original files are never changed.

5. No cloud services for your footage

The App has no:

  • User accounts or registration
  • Cloud storage or processing of your footage
  • Analytics or tracking services
  • Advertising networks or ad SDKs
  • Third-party data sharing

6. Permissions

The App runs as a standard Windows desktop application. It needs read access to the folders you point it at, write access to the output folder you choose, and the ability to launch the bundled ffmpeg.exe. It does not require administrator rights for normal use.

7. Updates, CCTV Converter Pro and links that open in your browser or the Microsoft Store

A small number of buttons in the App launch links in whatever app your system uses to open them — for example:

  • The footer links to davidarthur.app and info@davidarthur.app open your default browser or email client.
  • If you click "Rate on Store" in the one-time rating prompt, the App opens the App's review page in the Microsoft Store.

These actions happen only when you click those specific buttons.

The App also checks whether a newer version is available — automatically each time it starts, and whenever you choose "Check for updates". The Microsoft Store edition asks the Store's own built-in update service and, only when the Store reports an update, also reads a small version file hosted at davidarthur.app to see whether that update is required; the direct-download edition checks the same version file. A check sends only an ordinary web request (which, like any web request, includes your IP address) and transmits none of your settings, files, or footage. You can skip an optional update. If an update is marked as required, the App installs it by itself — after any conversion or export in progress has finished — and then reopens; if it cannot install it, it asks you to update before you carry on using it. If the check fails or you are offline, nothing happens. Aside from the actions described above, the App makes no background network requests.

CCTV Converter Pro. Pro is optional. You can buy it in two ways:

  • Microsoft Store: the purchase is made and billed by Microsoft, in the Microsoft Store's own window and under Microsoft's privacy statement (privacy.microsoft.com). To know whether you own Pro, the App asks the Microsoft Store's licensing service on your PC; Microsoft tells the App only which Pro plan you own and until when. We receive Microsoft's usual sales reports, which do not identify you to us.
  • Card or PayPal (online checkout, Paddle): purchases made through our online checkout are sold and processed by Paddle.com, our reseller and Merchant of Record. Paddle collects the details needed to take payment, charge the right tax and prevent fraud (such as your name, email address, payment details, billing country and postcode, and your IP address) and is an independent controller of them under its own privacy notice (paddle.com/legal/privacy). Paddle may use cookies or similar technologies needed for checkout and fraud prevention. We never see your full payment details. Paddle shares with us what we need to deliver and support your order: the order, customer and subscription identifiers, what was bought, the billing country, the subscription's status and dates, and, in its dashboard, your email address so that we can answer questions about your order.

If you buy with card or PayPal, the App creates a random install identifier (it contains nothing about you or your PC) and uses it to ask our licence server, at api.duovox.net, for your licence: when you buy or activate Pro, when you enter a CCTV Converter code, and from time to time while a subscription is running so that a renewal or cancellation reaches the App. Our server keeps the identifiers Paddle gives the order, the customer and any subscription, the product bought, the billing country, the subscription's status and paid-up date and the activation code we issue, linked to the install identifiers of the PCs that use it. It keeps these for as long as the purchase is active and then for up to 7 years for our tax and accounting records. These requests, like any web request, include your IP address, which our server uses only to deliver the request and to limit abuse; it never receives your settings, files or footage.

8. Data retention

All data the App writes is on your device for as long as you keep the App installed. In the direct-download edition, deleting %LOCALAPPDATA%\CctvConverter\ removes everything. Uninstalling the Microsoft Store edition removes it all; the direct-download edition's uninstaller removes the caches and logs but keeps your small settings file, so a reinstall remembers your folders.

9. Support emails and general correspondence

If you email us directly — for example, to ask for help with the App or to report a problem — we will receive your message, including your email address and anything you choose to tell us. We use that information only to respond to you and to keep a brief record of the conversation so we can help you again later.

  • We do not add your email address to any marketing list.
  • We do not share your email or its contents with any third party.
  • We keep support emails for up to 2 years, then delete them.
  • Email is handled by standard infrastructure with encrypted connections.

If you would like us to delete any correspondence with us, email info@davidarthur.app with "Delete my data" in the subject line and we will comply within 30 days.

10. Your rights

Unless you buy CCTV Converter Pro through our online checkout, we (David Arthur Software) do not hold any personal data about you in respect of using the App, and there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete on a server. If you did buy through our online checkout, you can ask us for a copy of the order record described in section 7, or to correct or delete it (we may need to keep what tax and accounting law requires), by emailing info@davidarthur.app. For the payment details Paddle holds, contact Paddle as described in its privacy notice; for Microsoft Store purchases, use your Microsoft account privacy dashboard.

For data the App stores locally, you can:

  • Access / view: open %LOCALAPPDATA%\CctvConverter\config.json in a text editor.
  • Rectify: change source/output folders inside the App.
  • Erase: delete the %LOCALAPPDATA%\CctvConverter\ folder, or uninstall the App.

If you believe we have handled your data incorrectly and are not happy with our response, you have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or by phone on 0303 123 1113.

11. Website cookies and local storage

The davidarthur.app website is a static site. It uses a web analytics service that counts visits without cookies and without tracking you across other websites, and it sets no advertising cookies. It also stores a small language-preference value in your browser's local storage so the site can remember the language you selected; this never leaves your device.
