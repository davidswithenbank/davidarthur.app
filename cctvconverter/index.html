CCTV Converter

Convert and view your Chinese CCTV camera files.

Free • No ads • No accounts • Works offline • 16 languages

Most Chinese DVRs save footage in proprietary formats that no normal player will open. CCTV Converter detects the format and converts to standard MP4 in parallel for big batches, optionally burning a date/time clock into the video for evidence use. New in v2.0: trim and enhance clips in a built-in editor, compare up to six cameras in sync, and automatically pull out just the moments with motion — all on your own machine, in your language.

How It Works

1

Pick the SD card or folder

Drag a folder onto the window or click Browse. CCTV Converter walks the date subfolders and finds every video file.

2

Filter and select

Hide short clips, narrow by date or time of day, search by name, or just tick whole days. Folders with no matches hide themselves.

3

Convert & review

Stream-copy to MP4 in seconds (no quality loss). Preview, scrub frame by frame, snapshot moments, and merge a day's clips into one continuous video.

Features

🔍

Smart Format Detection

Magic-byte detection identifies Xiongmai, Dahua, Hikvision, raw H.264/H.265 streams and 20+ other formats automatically — no guessing required.

⚡

Lossless Conversion

Stream-copy where possible (no re-encoding) means a 30-second clip converts in well under a second with zero quality loss. Falls back to a clean H.264 re-encode if needed.

🚀

Parallel Conversion

Convert up to 8 files at once on multi-core machines. A full day of recordings that used to take minutes now finishes in seconds. Adjustable from 1 to 8 concurrent jobs.

🕘

Date / Time Burn-in

Optional clock overlay burned right into the converted MP4 — perfect for insurance claims, court evidence and incident reports where timestamped footage is required.

🌐

16 Languages

Full UI in English, Indonesian, Vietnamese, Thai, Filipino, Chinese (Simplified), Japanese, Korean, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Russian, Turkish, Ukrainian and Arabic (right-to-left). Auto-detects your Windows language; switch any time with one click.

🔍

Powerful Filters

Hide clips below 10 / 20 / 30 / 45 seconds or 1 to 5 minutes. Filter by date, time of day or filename. Folders without matches collapse out of the way automatically.

📹

Built-in Preview

Play, pause, frame-by-frame stepping, variable speed (0.5×–8×), volume control, and a fullscreen mode with draggable controls so you can see what's underneath.

📷

Frame Snapshots

Capture any frame as JPEG or PNG — perfect for incident reports, social posts or sharing a moment without the whole clip.

🔗

Merge to Single MP4

Stitch a day's clips, or any selection across multiple folders, into one continuous video in chronological order. Useful for handing footage to insurers or police.

📝

CSV Export

Export the full file list with filename, size, duration, resolution, format and timestamps — handy for cataloguing or evidence trails.

📥

Drag & Drop

Drag any folder onto the window to scan it instantly. Last source and output folders are remembered between sessions.

✊

Cancellable Operations

Stop a long scan or batch conversion at any moment. Partial files are cleaned up automatically so nothing's left in a bad state.

📁

Optional Cleanup

Tick "Delete source files after successful conversion" to reclaim SD-card or drive space as you go — with confirmation.
