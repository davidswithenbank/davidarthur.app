Files Your Player Won't Open?

If your camera came from a Chinese vendor, the SD card is probably full of .ps, .dav or .264 files that VLC and Windows Media Player refuse. CCTV Converter detects the format from each file's signature bytes and turns it into a clean MP4 your phone, browser or any video editor can open.

Features

🔍

Smart Format Detection

Magic-byte detection identifies Xiongmai, Dahua, Hikvision, raw H.264/H.265 streams and 20+ other formats automatically — no guessing required.

⚡

Lossless Conversion

Stream-copy where possible (no re-encoding) means a 30-second clip converts in well under a second with zero quality loss. Falls back to a clean H.264 re-encode if needed.

🔍

Powerful Filters

Hide clips below 10 / 20 / 30 / 45 seconds or 1 to 5 minutes. Filter by date, time of day or filename. Folders without matches collapse out of the way automatically.

📹

Built-in Preview

Play, pause, frame-by-frame stepping, variable speed (0.5×–8×), volume control, and a fullscreen mode with draggable controls so you can see what's underneath.

📷

Frame Snapshots

Capture any frame as JPEG or PNG — perfect for incident reports, social posts or sharing a moment without the whole clip.

🔗

Merge to Single MP4

Stitch a day's clips, or any selection across multiple folders, into one continuous video in chronological order. Useful for handing footage to insurers or police.

📝

CSV Export

Export the full file list with filename, size, duration, resolution, format and timestamps — handy for cataloguing or evidence trails.

Drag & Drop

Drag any folder onto the window to scan it instantly. Last source and output folders are remembered between sessions.
