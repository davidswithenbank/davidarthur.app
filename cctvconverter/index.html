CCTV Converter

Convert and view your Chinese CCTV camera files.

Free • No ads • No accounts • Works offline • 16 languages

Most Chinese DVRs save footage in proprietary formats that no normal player will open. CCTV Converter detects the format and converts to standard MP4 in parallel for big batches, optionally burning a date/time clock into the video. New in v2.2: a simpler start, a copy ready for phone and WhatsApp, an evidence package for handing footage over, and raw files that play at the right speed — all on your own machine, in your language.

A Simpler Start

Drop a recorder folder or some video files onto the window, or use Choose folder… or Choose files…. Ready for Phone & WhatsApp

Save a small copy (16 MB or less) that plays on phones and can be sent straight into a chat.

Evidence Package

One folder to hand over: a checked copy of the original recording, the MP4 as edited, a report listing what was changed, SHA-256 checksums and a README.

Raw Files at the Right Speed

Raw .264 and .265 files often don't store their speed. The app works it out from the recording times in the file names, and you can set the camera's frame rate yourself under Recorded at.

Join & Split Recordings

When recordings carry straight on from each other, a banner offers to join them into one video. A file that holds several cameras can be split into one video per camera.

Files Your Player Won't Open?

If your camera came from a Chinese vendor, the SD card is probably full of .ps, .dav or .264 files that VLC and Windows Media Player refuse. CCTV Converter detects the format from each file's signature bytes and turns it into a clean MP4 your phone, browser or any video editor can open.

How It Works

1

Pick the SD card or folder

Drag a folder or some video files onto the window, or click Choose folder…. CCTV Converter walks the date subfolders and finds every video file.

2

Filter and select

Hide short clips, narrow by date or time of day, search by name, or just tick whole days. Files that don't match are hidden and unticked, and folders with no matches hide themselves.

3

Convert & review

Stream-copy to MP4 in seconds (no quality loss). Preview, scrub frame by frame, snapshot moments, and merge a day's clips into one continuous video.
