Refund Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This page sets out our position on refunds and returns for all David Arthur Software products. In short: paid app purchases made in the Microsoft Store are handled by Microsoft, and DuoVox and CCTV Converter Pro purchases made through our online checkout are handled by Paddle.com, our online reseller. Refunds are governed by their published policies.

1. Free Apps

The following app is currently a free download:

  • QuietTime — Free on the Microsoft Store and Google Play.

As this app is free, no purchase is made and no refund is applicable.

2. DuoVox and CCTV Converter

DuoVox has a free tier and paid plans:

  • The free version is free to use — no purchase is made and no refund is applicable. On Windows it shows only our own in-app messages about DuoVox; no third-party advertising network is used.
  • Paid plans and pay-as-you-go credit are sold through the app store you buy from (see sections 3 and 4) or, where it is offered, through our online checkout (see section 5). Refunds are handled by that store, or by Paddle.com for online checkout purchases, under its own published refund policy.

CCTV Converter is free to download, and playing and converting files are free — no purchase is made and no refund is applicable. CCTV Converter Pro, its optional paid upgrade, is sold through the Microsoft Store (see section 4) or through our online checkout (see section 5). Refunds are handled by Microsoft, or by Paddle.com for online checkout purchases, under its own published refund policy. A refunded purchase switches Pro off.

3. Google Play

None of our apps or in-app products is currently sold through Google Play (QuietTime is free there). If that changes, refund requests will be handled directly by Google, as set out in Google Play's official refund policy.

4. Refunds via the Microsoft Store

If you purchased an app or in-app product via the Microsoft Store, refund requests are handled directly by Microsoft. Full details and the request flow are available in the Microsoft Store refund policy.

5. DuoVox and CCTV Converter Pro purchases through our online checkout

Our online checkout is provided by Paddle.com, our online reseller. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all orders placed through our online checkout, and refunds for those orders follow Paddle's refund policy. In short:

  • Asking for a refund: you can request a refund within 14 days of a purchase at paddle.net (choose "Request refund"), or through the link in the receipt email Paddle sent you. Paddle reviews each request under its refund policy.
  • Your legal rights: consumers may also have a legal right to cancel a purchase within a set period under the law of their country. Nothing in this policy takes those rights away.
  • Where the money goes: an approved refund is paid back to the payment method you used.
  • What a refund ends: when a purchase is refunded in full, the plan, the Pay-As-You-Go credit or the CCTV Converter Pro it bought ends at once.

Cancelling a subscription: you can cancel at any time using the link in your receipt email from Paddle, at paddle.net, or by asking us at info@duovox.net (DuoVox) or info@davidarthur.app (CCTV Converter). You keep your paid features until the end of the period you have paid for, and you are not charged again. Cancelling stops future payments; it does not by itself refund the current period.

Purchases through our online checkout are made with David Arthur Software Ltd (company number 14318326), registered office Independence House, Millfield Lane, Nether Poppleton, York, YO26 6PH, United Kingdom, with Paddle.com as reseller. The full DuoVox Refund Policy, the same text as on duovox.net, is at davidarthur.app/duovox/refunds/; where it and this page differ on anything concerning DuoVox, the DuoVox Refund Policy prevails.

6. Statutory Rights

Nothing in this policy affects your statutory rights under applicable consumer protection law (including, where relevant, the UK Consumer Rights Act 2015 and the EU Consumer Rights Directive). If a paid app is faulty, not as described, or otherwise does not meet the standards required by law, you remain entitled to the remedies provided by that law — usually exercised through the store where you bought the app.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy:

  • General: info@davidarthur.app
  • DuoVox: support@duovox.net

For refund requests on a paid purchase, please go directly to the Microsoft Store, or to paddle.net for an online checkout purchase — we cannot process those refunds on their behalf.
