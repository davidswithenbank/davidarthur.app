4.4 Accuracy and Limitations

CCTV Converter detects video formats from signature bytes and converts to standard MP4. It supports Xiongmai IMKH, Dahua DHAV, Hikvision, raw H.264/H.265 and 20+ other formats. Some vendor-specific encrypted formats (for example Hikvision .crypt backups) cannot be decoded by any third-party tool and will be flagged as unconvertible. The optional date/time overlay reads each file's modification timestamp and is provided for convenience; if accurate evidence-grade timestamping is critical, verify the source timestamps independently. Converted, edited, or trimmed output should not be relied upon as the sole forensic or legal record; where footage may be used for insurance, legal, or law-enforcement purposes, always retain and verify the original source files.
